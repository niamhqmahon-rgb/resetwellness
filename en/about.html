about us.

A studio with a simple idea: skincare that takes its time, for your skin and for you.

Skincare can't always be rushed. It's time to slow down and reset.

Reset was founded in 2026 by Laura Guegan & Lysa Gouffie in Berlin. After years in the busy and dynamic skincare industry, we wanted a place where treatments don’t run on the clock.

Today we are a small team who work with a short list of carefully chosen products and always explain what we do and, more importantly, why.
