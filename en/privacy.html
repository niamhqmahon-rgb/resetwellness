2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Hostinger (Hostinger International Ltd., 61 Lordou Vironos Street, 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus). When you visit, technical data is collected automatically (IP address, date and time, page visited, browser and operating system). 3. Contact and appointment requests

When you contact us by email or by phone, we process your details (name, email, phone if given, preferred date, message) to handle your request and arrange appointments. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (precontractual steps) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Your data is deleted once your request is completed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Health data (skin consultation)

For safe treatment we ask about skin conditions, allergies, medication or pregnancy before your first appointment. This is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. We process it only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), only in the studio and only for your treatment. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

5. Cookies and embedded content

We only use technically necessary cookies (Section 25(2) TDDDG). Our contact page includes a map from Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). The map only loads when you click “Show map” (Section 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Google then receives data such as your IP address and may set cookies; data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the Data Privacy Framework between the EU and the USA. Your consent applies only to that page visit: the map is not loaded again until you click once more. Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Other external content (e.g. [BOOKING TOOL]) likewise only loads after you actively consent.

6. Retention

We only store personal data for as long as necessary for the purpose concerned. Invoices and accounting records are kept for the statutory periods (Section 147 AO, Section 257 HGB).

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
